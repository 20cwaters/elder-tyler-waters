About the mission

The is based in Natal, the capital of the state of Rio Grande do Norte, on the northeastern corner of Brazil. The region is part of the Nordeste, the country's northeast.

Natal sits on the Atlantic, with long beaches and big sand dunes along the coast. It is warm and sunny nearly all year, cooled by the ocean breeze, and inland the land becomes drier countryside.

How a mission works

What is the MTC?

The Missionary Training Center. Missionaries learning a new language spend their first weeks there studying the language and practising teaching before they head out to their actual mission. Tyler started at the one in Provo, Utah, and went from there to Brazil.

What is a transfer?

Roughly every six weeks the mission president reassigns missionaries. Tyler may stay put or be moved to a new town, and he may get a new companion either way. Transfer news usually shows up in the weekly email, and the Where He Is page gets updated when it does.

What is a companion?

Missionaries are always paired up. Companions live, study, and teach together for the length of a transfer or longer. You will see a lot of names come and go over two years.

What is a P-day?

Preparation day — one day a week for laundry, groceries, sightseeing, and writing home. That is when the weekly email arrives, and when he is allowed to call or video chat with his family.
